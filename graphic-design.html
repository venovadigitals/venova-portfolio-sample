FAQ

Common Questions

What's included in a brand identity package?

A logo, colour palette, typography selection, and a brand guideline document so anyone creating content for you stays consistent.

Can you redesign an existing logo?

Yes, we can refresh or fully redesign an existing logo while keeping (or intentionally changing) brand recognition — your call.

Do you design for both print and digital?

Yes, we deliver print-ready files (CMYK, high-resolution) alongside web-optimised digital assets.
