SAG Drone Technologies — Full Website Build

A Venova Digitals case study

Visit Live Site ↗ Start a Similar Project
Project Overview

The Brief

SAG Drone Technologies needed more than a brochure site — they needed a full platform: a product catalogue customers could browse and order from, a way to publish drone-regulation content in both Telugu and English, and pages that loaded fast on the mobile connections most of their customers actually use.

Client
SAG Drone Technologies
Service
Web Design
Tools & Stack
Supabase, Vercel, vanilla JS, Cloudinary
What We Did

Key Highlights

  • Product catalogue with clean per-product URLs, dynamically routed and pulled from a Supabase database with JSON-LD schema on every product page
  • In-page cart with a slide-in drawer and direct WhatsApp checkout — no separate payment gateway needed for their sales flow
  • A bilingual blog (Telugu/English toggle) with an inline article reader, publishing content on DGCA drone regulations, RPTO training, and agri-drone spraying
  • A full Lighthouse performance pass: Cloudinary image transforms, responsive images, lazy-loading, non-blocking font loading, and a throttled scroll-spy handler
  • An accessibility audit and fix pass: accessible button labels, larger touch targets, corrected contrast, and sequential heading order
