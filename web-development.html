FAQ

Common Questions

How long does it take to build a website?

Most business websites are ready in 2–4 weeks depending on scope. Ecommerce or custom web-app builds typically take 4–8 weeks.

Will my website be SEO-friendly from launch?

Yes. Every site we build includes on-page SEO — meta titles, descriptions, schema markup, an XML sitemap, and fast load times — so it's ready to be indexed and ranked from day one.

Do you build ecommerce websites?

Yes, we build ecommerce platforms with product catalogues, cart, checkout, and payment gateway integration for businesses across India.
